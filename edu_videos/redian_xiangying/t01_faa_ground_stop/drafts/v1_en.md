## s3 Why touching it is the risky part
- l41: Here's how we see it.
- l42: Air traffic control never stops. No rebooting it like a laptop.
- l43: You swap it mid-flight, while the old parts keep running.
- l44: That overlap is when it's most fragile.
- l45: The older it gets, the fewer people understand it, or dare touch it.
- l46: So changes get riskier. So nobody touches it. So it gets older.
- l47: We'd call that the interest on technical debt.
