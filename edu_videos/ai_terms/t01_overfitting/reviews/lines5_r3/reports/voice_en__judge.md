## 驳回
- l08, b's rewrite "Let's test this with ten points. They're our training data, our practice questions.": it uses "test" for the practice data three lines before l17 introduces "the test data, our exam". That blurs the train/test split, which is the whole point of the chapter. "Test this" also has no clear object. Use the 补充问题 rewrite.
- l08, a's rewrite "Let's try it. Here are ten points we collected.": it is longer than the original (it adds three words without cutting anything), so it breaks the rule that a rewrite must be no longer. "Try it" is also vaguer than "see it happen" about what is coming.
- l31, a's and b's rewrite "checked fifty million search terms against … eleven hundred flu data points": "checked against" means compared. It drops the idea of picking the best-fitting terms, which is what the original "sifted … to match" says and what the source says ("find the best matches among 50 million search terms to fit 1152 data points"). That selection is the mechanism behind l32 and the overfitting verdict in l33. Keep the original.
- l30, a's rewrite "estimate how many doctor visits were for flu-like symptoms": "how many" turns a proportion into a count. GFT estimated the share of visits for flu-like illness (claims.json), so the author's I75 reason holds against this wording.
- l39, a's rewrite "Second, get more varied data, so noise and coincidences…": it drops "more data", so the advice shrinks to variety alone, and the step is "collect more data, and more varied data". b's version keeps both and is the acceptable optional one, but the original is not must-fix.
- l06, a's rewrite "The workbook is the data the model learns from, and the misprints are the noise.": it is longer than the original (16 words against 15). It also drops "what we call", which is where the term "noise" is first introduced, so the rewrite treats a new term as already known. b's retell hears the original as natural. Keep it.
- b's V1 score of 1: the cited evidence (l30, l31) is two accurate 建议-level density spots with no strict line involved. That supports a 2, not a 1.
- a's R score of 2: it treats I65 (l08) as an accepted retention, but a strict chapter-transition problem is still open. See the R ruling.
