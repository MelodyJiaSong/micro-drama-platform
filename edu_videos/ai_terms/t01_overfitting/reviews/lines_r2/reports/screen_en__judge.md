## 裁决

Counting rulings (the author follows these; they apply to every screen):

Counting ruling 1, agenda: NOT a breach of the 4-block limit. A list, table or row of cards counts as ONE block when its items share one style and one grammatical role, each item is at most 6 words, and there are at most 6 items. Index markers (01–06, ①–③), arrows and divider rules are not words. The agenda card is therefore 2 blocks ("In this episode" plus one 6-row list) and 19 words, on screen for 14 s (about 1.4 words/s). The 22-word screen cap still applies to everything, so a list cannot be used to slip extra text onto a screen. The same rule covers the l15–l22 score table, the l26 mapping rows and the l52–l55 card row.

Counting ruling 2, chart labels: NOT exempt. "Chart ticks" means tick values only (the axis numbers 1–9). Zone names (Underfit / Just right / Overfit), curve names (Practice error / Exam error), axis titles, bar-segment labels (Train / Valid. / Test) and in-chart callouts ("Exam points", "Noise: random wobble") are words a viewer reads: every one of them counts toward the 22-word cap. For the 4-block cap, all labels attached to one chart count together as ONE block (the chart); keep each label to 3 words or fewer. Under this rule l31–l34 pass (13 / 15 / 19 / 20 words, at most 4 blocks) and l35 is the only chart screen that fails (29 words, 5 blocks; see 补充问题).

Companion ruling: as the extraction totals do, and as the round-1 judge treated navigation (I4), the small section eyebrow, the l42–l50 case tabs, source lines and the end-card footer credits are exempt from BOTH caps. An eyebrow qualifies only as a small caps label of 5 words or fewer. A table column header ("PRACTICE ERROR · MEAN SQUARED MISS", "PRACTICE / EXAM") is part of the table, so its words count.
