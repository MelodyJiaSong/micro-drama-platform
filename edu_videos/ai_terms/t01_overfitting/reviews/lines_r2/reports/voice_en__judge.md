- a l45 rewrite ("it overshot in 100 of 108 weeks"): writing it in digits does not fix the spoken form. The script's spoken form {100|a hundred} of {108|a hundred and eight} would be read the same way. "Overshot" also drops what was being estimated. Use "a hundred out of a hundred and eight" (see 补充问题).
- a l14 rewrite ("how far the points sit from the line, on average"): it repeats a suggestion the author already declined last round, for a valid reason. The on-screen error values are squared-and-averaged, so "on average" would misstate them. Use b's direction, with a clear referent: "To score it, we square how far each point misses the line, then average." (14 words, same as the original)
