## 驳回
- l41 (b's rewrite): "trusting them as much as the answers" muddles the analogy, because in l04 the misprints are inside the answers. It also drops "real patterns", which ties back to l07/l09. Use a's rewrite: "Overfitting is memorizing the misprints and mistaking them for real patterns." Adding "like" after "is" is optional.
- l31 (b's rewrite): "Google searched fifty million terms" sounds like Google ran 50 million searches. It also drops "At first", which sets up l34 "Later versions". And "measurements" breaks the link to l32 "far more terms than data points". a's "flu records" breaks the same link. Use: "At first, it sifted fifty million search terms to match about eleven hundred flu data points." (16 words. The screen still shows 1,152.)
- l34 (a's rewrite): "Over a hundred and eight weeks" can be heard as "more than 108 weeks", which brings back the ear problem it was meant to fix. b's version runs longer when spoken and gives the count twice ("almost every week" plus the numbers). Use: "Later versions missed too. Out of a hundred and eight weeks, they guessed too high in a hundred." (18 words)
- l35 (a's rewrite): "So how do you stop it? There are three common fixes." drops "catch", but l37 "catch overfitting early" depends on it, and a validation set detects overfitting rather than fixing it. The 必改 flag stands. Use: "So how do you spot it and stop it? Here are three common ways." (14 words, same as the original)
- l36 (a's rewrite): It is longer than the original (14 vs 13 words). The issue itself is valid: the screen says "a mock exam" / "Mock", and "practice exam" blurs with "practice questions". Use: "First, set aside some data as a mock exam, called a validation set."
- l39 (a's rewrite): "get more, and more varied, data" puts a written-style parenthetical in the middle of the phrase, which is harder to say aloud than the original. If a change is wanted: "Second, get more data, and more varied data, so flukes are less likely to look like patterns."
- l40 (a's rewrite): "Third, a rule that penalizes..." is a fragment, and with "the model" gone, "it won't chase noise" has nothing to refer to. The original is fine spoken, and b's retell marks it natural.
- l30 (a's rewrite): "doctor visits were for the flu" is less accurate than the original. Google Flu Trends estimated visits for flu-like illness, not confirmed flu. If changed, keep "for flu-like symptoms".
- l12 (a's rewrite): "Each point the line misses counts against it" drops the distance idea ("farther → more"), which the error picture depends on. Use b's rewrite. The flag itself stands, at 必改 level (b), not 建议 (a).
- l38 (b's rewrite and V4 point): "To be honest" is not filler. It marks an admission that the video's own experiment cut a corner, and a lists it as a human touch to keep. Keep the original.
- l07 (a's "cut or merge"): Cutting it is not a drop-in fix. l07 is the analogy's payoff and closes the chapter, and l41 calls back to it on purpose. Keep it.
- "A small experiment" card (a's 记录): The viewer never sees or hears the section name "Three ways to learn". The card is idiomatic and fits the content, so there is no mismatch to fix.
