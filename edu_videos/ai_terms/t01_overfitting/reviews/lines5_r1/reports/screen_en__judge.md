The red ③ row and green ② row already show the comparison. Shorter option: "Exam error: 25× the gentle curve's" (6 words). a's "Wiggly curve: 25× the exam error" is rejected (see 驳回).

## 驳回
- l32 (b's rewrite "Coincidental winter peaks")：It is a 3-word noun stack that reads stiffly aloud, which is the P4 failure mode, and it drops the subject. Use a's "Some terms just peak in winter" (6 words). It fixes both the count and the missing noun after "Some".
- l21 (b's 必改 and "Noise ≠ pattern")："Memorized noise won't repeat" is a 4-word claim headline, normal keynote style, and not a narration sentence. b's rewrite brings in a symbol that cannot be read aloud (the same fault as l20). It also changes the point from "won't repeat on new data" to "noise is not a pattern". Keep the original.
- l05 (b's 必改 and "Changed questions → confusion")：The original is a 6-word story beat beside the student illustration and reads naturally. The rewrite is a stiff noun-plus-arrow stack. At most a 记录 for the "and he's lost" echo.
- outro card_s10 (b's 必改 and "Noise mistaken for patterns")：A 6-word takeaway that calls back the misprint analogy is the end card's job. It stays within limits and matches the voice_en judge's ruling on l41. The rewrite drops the callback. Only the contrast fix stands (补充问题).
- l03 (b's rewrite "Training flukes → weaker predictions")：It is a noun stack, and "training flukes" is ambiguous (flukes during training?). See the gerund rewrite in 补充问题.
- l22–l23 (b's "Underfit: poor practice + exam")：It uses "+" in a heading and drops "easy to spot / hard to catch", which is the point of l22–l23. The same applies to a's rewrite, which also drops the detectability contrast. a's claim that "aces" is slang is rejected too: "aces practice" is standard informal English and fine for this register. Keep both lines. Changing "fails both" is optional.
- l29 (b's "Pattern retained, noise removed / Overcompression: pattern lost")：Passive nouns replace natural imperatives. The original "Keep the pattern, drop the noise / Squeeze too hard: lose the pattern" is better slide English.
- l33 (b's "Flu signal + winter noise")：The quoted line is the researchers' own wording (Lazer et al., Science, 2014), so a quotation cannot be rewritten. Only the attribution line is weak. Use "Verdict: overfitting" (a's "Their verdict: overfitting" is also fine).
- l34 (b's "weeks overestimated" ambiguity)：After a huge "100 / 108", "weeks overestimated" reads naturally as "weeks with an overestimate". No native viewer will take it to mean the number of weeks was overestimated. Keep it.
- l36–l40 bar (b's "Training / Validation / Test")：It does not drop in. "Validation" will not fit the narrow purple segment at the current width, and the purple "Mock" is colour-linked to the purple "mock exam" on card ①. Keep "Mock", or use a's parallel verbs "Train / Validate / Test" only if the segment is widened.
- l20 (a's rewrite "Wiggly curve: 25× the exam error")：It is ambiguous: 25 times whose exam error? Use the judge's rewrite above.
- l27 (a's rewrite without the circled digits)：It drops the "compressed / no compression" tags, which are the point of chapter 03. Keep the tags. Fixing only the unnumbered first row is enough.
- c_problem (a's 建议 "What is overfitting?")："What overfitting is" is a natural chapter title, and it now pairs with "How to prevent overfitting". Keep it.
