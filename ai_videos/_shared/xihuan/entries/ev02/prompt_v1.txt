Original fantasy visual-effects concept frame for a live-action film. A single hooded caster seen from behind at three-quarter angle (face not visible) stands on a plain dark stone floor in an empty dark space, releasing one clearly readable magical effect. The effect's shape, color and material are the subject and fill most of the frame. Cinematic photoreal lighting where the magic is the main light source.

A hooded figure curled on the floor inside a sphere of absolute swirling black darkness that swallows all light, while from the very center of their chest a new bright metallic light is bursting outward, cracking the black sphere open with lines of light like an egg breaking. Death and rebirth, dramatic, cathartic.

Original design, not a recreation of any existing game spell effect. No text, no letters, no symbols that read as real writing, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no split panels.
