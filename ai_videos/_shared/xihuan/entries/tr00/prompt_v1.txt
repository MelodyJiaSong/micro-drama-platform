Original fantasy visual-effects concept frame for a live-action film. A single hooded caster seen from behind at three-quarter angle (face not visible) stands on a plain dark stone floor in an empty dark space, releasing one clearly readable magical effect. The effect's shape, color and material are the subject and fill most of the frame. Cinematic photoreal lighting where the magic is the main light source.

The hooded figure stands quietly in a dim grey space with no glow and no magic at all, only ordinary soft light on their worn wool cloak; at their feet a lump of dark raw clay rests on the stone floor, unformed. Calm, ordinary, full of unlit potential.

Original design, not a recreation of any existing game spell effect. No text, no letters, no symbols that read as real writing, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no split panels. No real-world religious, royal, national or occult symbols: no crosses or inverted crosses, no pentagrams or five-pointed stars, no triple-moon signs, no fleur-de-lis, no triangle-circle-line sigils, no swastika-like or real runic letters.
