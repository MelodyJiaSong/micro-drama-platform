# Edit brief — Portal Travel EP01 "One Day in Pompeii, 75 AD", Scene 3: The Bakery

This brief is given unchanged to both editors in the blind test. Reviewers judge both versions against it.

## Series vision

**Spend one whole day in another era and another city — as if you had really been there, seeing, tasting and hearing how ordinary people lived that day.** The host, Riley, is a cheerful American vlogger. Locals speak Latin; she does not understand it, guesses, and answers with body language. Tone: warm, curious, gently funny. Immersion matters more than speed: let the audience look around and take in the place.

## Scene 3 — early morning, a bakery with an open shop front

This is the first time Riley buys and eats something in the ancient city.

Story beats, in order:

1. **The bakery before any people:** a blindfolded donkey turns a stone mill, flour sifts down; the camera pans to the brick dome oven, where the baker Felix pulls a round loaf scored into eight wedges out of the fire.
2. **"Salve!"** Felix sees Riley and greets her. She tries to repeat it and gets the pronunciation wrong ("Sal-VAY"); he laughs. She holds up one finger, puts two small coins on the counter; he drops them in a clay bowl and pushes a loaf to her.
3. **Pre-sliced bread:** she snaps off one wedge along the scoring, steam rises. To camera, in the take: "Sliced bread. Before sliced bread."
4. **The taste:** chewing, nodding, in the take: "Dense. Smoky. Good."
5. **Asking the way:** Felix wraps the rest in linen; she hangs it on her belt and asks "Garum?" (fish sauce). Without looking up from his tray he rattles off in Latin: "Hac recta platea… ito ad dextram!" (Straight down this street… go to the right!)
6. **The wrong guess:** she thinks, turns to camera, in the take: "Left, then straight, then something. I'm going with left."
7. **Button:** she walks out and turns **left**. Felix opens his mouth to call her back, stops, shakes his head and laughs, and goes back to laying out bread. The audience knows she went the wrong way; the scene lands on his laugh.

## Deliverable

- One edited scene, 16:9, 1280×720, 24 fps, about 45–55 seconds.
- Use only the three clips above, with their own audio.
- Editing principles (house style):
  - Never cut inside a word; keep a breath around lines.
  - Every cut needs a reason: new information, a reaction, an eyeline or an action.
  - Keep the comic timing: the mispronunciation and his laugh, the wrong-way guess and his laugh.
  - This is immersive travel, not a fast drama: let establishing moments breathe, and hold at least 1.5 s at the end of the scene.
  - Do not drop story beats; do not reorder them unless it clearly helps.
