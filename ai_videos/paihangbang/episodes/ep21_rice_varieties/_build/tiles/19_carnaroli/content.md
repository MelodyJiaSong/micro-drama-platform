# #20 Carnaroli (Riso Carnaroli)

- Card: Carnaroli — Vercelli & Pavia, Italy, Italy; big text 'Long grain'
- Grain: Large medium-long grain · colour: Pearly white core · aroma: neutral · texture: creamy outside, firm 'al dente' core
- Fact: Created in 1945 by crossing Vialone and Lencino; its firm grains hold their shape in risotto.
