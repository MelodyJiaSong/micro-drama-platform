# #2 Yamada Nishiki (山田錦)

- Card: Yamada Nishiki — Hyogo, Japan, Japan; big text 'Short grain'
- Grain: Large short grain, big starchy core · colour: White (opaque heart) · aroma: neutral · texture: brewed, not eaten: milled down to its white core
- Fact: Developed in Hyogo in 1923. In 2014 Hyogo still grew 71% of Japan's crop of this sake rice.
