## WWD 描述（原文摘录，仅内部调研，不进成片）

> Ølmåfossen is an officially named waterfall of lofty stature but not terribly significant volume found in Norway's renowned Romsdalen.  Fed by a small glacier and a handful of modest lakes, the falls veil about 710 meters (2,330 feet) down the immense cliffs lining the famous Trollveggen - which feature the tallest unbroken cliffs in Europe (just not at the falls).  The falls are visible from the town of Marstein and are likely often confused as the more well-known but almost never recognized and now dry Mongefossen, which is located a few kilometers further to the east on the north wall of the valley.
> We attempted to survey this waterfall in June of 2011 only to be met with low clouds and rain, which obstructed all but the bottom 1/4 of the waterfall.  What could be seen was still rather impressive but without having seen the entire waterfall, we can't at this time present a full report on the stature and quality of the falls beyond speculation and information which we have received from other sources.
