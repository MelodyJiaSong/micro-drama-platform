## Article text (lead section)

Guangxi China Resources Tower or China Resources Centre Block A is a supertall skyscraper in Nanning, Guangxi, China. It rises 403 metres (1,322.2 ft) tall. Construction started in 2014 and opened on November 2, 2020. It was originally planned to be 445 m (1,460 ft) but was cancelled mid construction due to airspace restrictions.
