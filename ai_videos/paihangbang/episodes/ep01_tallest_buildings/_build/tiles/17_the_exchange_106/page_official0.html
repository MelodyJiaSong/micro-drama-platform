Frequently asked questions

Your guide to navigating Exchange 106, from business needs to daily convenience within the tower and TRX district.

Where is Exchange 106 located?

Exchange 106 is located within the Tun Razak Exchange (TRX) district in Kuala Lumpur, positioned alongside The Exchange TRX lifestyle destination and directly connected to the city’s wider transportation network.

How do I get to Exchange 106 by public transport?

Exchange 106 is directly accessible via the Tun Razak Exchange MRT Station, the only MRT interchange station in Kuala Lumpur serving both the Putrajaya and Kajang MRT lines.

How do I access Exchange 106 from The Exchange TRX mall?

There are several direct access points between The Exchange TRX mall and Exchange 106. Visitors may enter through the Level 1 walkway beside Sephora, the Level 2 connection next to Sun & Sand Sports (SSS), or through the lobby access near the Kenny Hills entrance at TRX Park. Direct escalator and lift access is also available via the TRX MRT station.

Is parking available within the building?

Yes. Exchange 106 provides premium parking facilities across multiple levels, including VIP parking on P1 and P2, with standard parking available from P3 to P8 for both tenants and visitors. The building offers over 2,000 parking bays, with additional connectivity to The Exchange TRX mall parking facilities through P7.

How do I enquire about office leasing opportunities?

For leasing enquiries and workspace availability, please contact leasing@exchange106.my and the leasing team will assist you further.

Exchange 106 is a Grade A commercial tower anchoring Tun Razak Exchange, Malaysia's International Financial Centre. Exchange 106 is home to a community where 90% of tenants are international businesses spanning finance, technology and beyond.

From technical services and security to concierge and housekeeping, Exchange 106 is managed entirely in-house, thus ensuring a premium experience across every level of operation.
