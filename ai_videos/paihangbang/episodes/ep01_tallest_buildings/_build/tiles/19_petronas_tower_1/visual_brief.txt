Petronas Tower 1 has a silver, strongly ribbed steel-and-glass facade, lobed shafts, repeated setbacks, and a narrow needle spire; the skybridge connects it to its nearly identical sister at mid-height. Classic views show both towers from KLCC Park and Lake Symphony or the opposite entrance plaza, with the podium visible. Its primary look-alike is Tower 2 itself, while nearby Menara Maxis, Four Seasons and Petronas Tower 3 must not be mistaken for either twin; retain both towers where individual numbering cannot be proven from the photograph.
