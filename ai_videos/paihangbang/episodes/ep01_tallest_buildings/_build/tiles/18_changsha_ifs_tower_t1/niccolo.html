Direction
Niccolo Changsha is situated a short 40 minute drive from Changsha’s Huanghua International Airport and 30 minutes from the high speed railway terminus, Changsha South Station.

Nearby Attractions

Changsha IFS (Direct access from the hotel)
A city-centre landmark

Huangxing Road Pedestrian Street (5 minutes' walk)
Neon-lit night scenes and a paradise for food lovers

Fengying Xili Lane (10 minutes' walk)
Boutique shops and relaxing cafes.

Dufu River Pavilion (12 minutes' walk)
A landmark by the Xiangjiang River, blending night views with poetic charm.

Chaozong Street (8 minutes by taxi)
A revitalized historic street lined with trendy shops.

Orange Isle (10 minutes by taxi)
A scenic islet home to the iconic Young Mao Zedong Statue.

Yuelu Academy (15 minutes by taxi)
A millennia-old institution of learning with an uninterrupted legacy of scholarship.

Hunan Museum (15 minutes by taxi)
Home to precious relics from the Mawangdui Han Tombs, including the Plain Unlined Gauze Gown and T-shaped Silk Painting.

Xie Zilong Photography Museum & Li Zijian Art Museum (12 minutes by taxi)
Minimalist architectural masterpieces standing serenely by the picturesque Swan Lake. Free entry to the diverse allure of Chinese oil painting and visual arts.
