## Article text (lead section)

The Iconic Tower is a mixed-use supertall skyscraper in The New Capital of Egypt. With a total structural height of 394 metres (1,293 ft), it is both the tallest building and the tallest structure in Africa. It has 77 floors, mostly for office use, and is one of 20 towers being built as part of the central business district in the new capital city. The total area of the tower exceeds 265,000 m2 (2,850,000 sq ft).
