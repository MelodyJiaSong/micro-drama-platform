Tianjin CTF Finance Centre is a slender blue-silver glass tower whose broad base tapers through softly curved sides into a rounded, multi-lobed crown. Long sweeping ribs trace elongated curves along its facade, unlike Guangzhou CTF's rectilinear setbacks. It stands above the comparatively low and open TEDA/Binhai district; a clear three-quarter view from nearby roads or landscaped plazas best shows the complete silhouette. Avoid confusing it with the boxy Goldin Finance 117 elsewhere in Tianjin, the Guangzhou CTF sister project, or the pointed Lotte World Tower.
