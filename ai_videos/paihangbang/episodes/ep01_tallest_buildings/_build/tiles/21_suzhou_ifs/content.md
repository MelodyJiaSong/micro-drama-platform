## Article text (lead section)

Suzhou International Financial Square (Chinese: 苏州国际金融中心) is a supertall skyscraper designed by Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates in the Suzhou Industrial Park, Jiangsu, located to the east of Jinji Lake. It is the tallest building in Suzhou. It is a multi-purpose building which includes apartments, hotels and offices.
