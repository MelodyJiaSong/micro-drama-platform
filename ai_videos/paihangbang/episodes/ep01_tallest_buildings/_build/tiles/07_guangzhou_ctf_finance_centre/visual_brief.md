Guangzhou CTF Finance Centre is a pale, vertically ribbed glass-and-terracotta tower with a rectangular shaft, successive asymmetric setbacks and a tall split-height crown. It rises beside K11 and Guangzhou Library on the east side of Huacheng Square in Zhujiang New Town; views across the square or from the Pearl River show its whole profile best. Distinguish it from the rounded, dark Guangzhou IFC West Tower, the lattice-waisted Canton Tower, and the smoothly tapered Tianjin CTF tower.
