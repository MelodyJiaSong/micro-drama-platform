## Article text (lead section)

China Merchants Bank Tower Global HQ (Chinese: 招银环球金融中心) is a mixed-use skyscraper building complex in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. Built between 2019 and 2025, the complex consists of five towers with the tallest one (Main Tower) standing at 393 m (1,289 ft) tall with 77 floors which is the third tallest building in Shenzhen.
