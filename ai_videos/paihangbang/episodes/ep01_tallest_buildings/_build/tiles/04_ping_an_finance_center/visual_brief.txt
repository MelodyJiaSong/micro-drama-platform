Ping An Finance Center is a slender silver-grey skyscraper in Shenzhen Futian, defined by strong stainless-steel corner ribs, long vertical facade lines and a faceted pyramidal crown without an antenna. Classic views from the Civic Center/Lianhuashan direction or nearby streets show it dominating the dense central business district, ideally with its base and crown visible. Distinguish it from the much shorter Ping An South Tower, the rounded KK100 in Luohu, the needle-topped Shun Hing Square, and the tapered China Resources Headquarters near Shenzhen Bay.
