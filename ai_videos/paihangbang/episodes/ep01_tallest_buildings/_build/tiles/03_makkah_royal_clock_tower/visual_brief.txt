The Makkah Royal Clock Tower is the central, tallest tower of the Abraj Al Bait complex: a broad green-glass shaft with tan framing, four enormous clock faces, Arabic calligraphy and a gold crescent-topped spire. It rises immediately south of the Grand Mosque, surrounded by shorter stepped hotel towers. The classic view is frontal or slightly oblique from the mosque plaza, showing the central tower from podium to crescent; do not confuse the flanking Abraj Al Bait hotels or nearby Jabal Omar towers with the ranked tower.
