## 1. 口径

- **主值**：成年雄性典型（平均）体重，kg；副显示 lb（×2.20462，取整到 10 lb，2,000 lb 以上取整到 100 lb）。屏上 1,000 kg 以上取整到 50 kg，以下照出处原值。
- **只给范围的取中点**（`kind = range midpoint`），在下表标出。排序用原值。
- **收录**：IUCN Red List 现行分类的现存野生物种；不收家畜 / 家养型、海兽、鳄类；收河马、北极熊。亚种不单列。
- **国旗**：该物种野外个体数最多的国家；**大洲**按国旗国家的 UN M49 分区（俄罗斯 → Europe）。
- **徽章**：IUCN 类别 NT / VU / EN / CR；LC 不显示。**标签**：野外数量、「Up to ~X kg」（出处给出的最大值：范围上限、个别大个体或推算值，象类为按肩高推算，不是称重记录；独立审片 M6）；非洲森林象另标「Estimate: none weighed」。
- **口径统一（独立审片 M2）**：凡出处只给雄性体重范围的，一律取该范围中点（棕熊 SDZ 雄性 130–550 → 340 入榜）；非洲水牛主值改用《Handbook of the Mammals of the World》雄性 500–900 的中点 700（M1）；gaur 用 Walker's 雄性 588–1,500 的中点 1,044（经维基转读，secondary；复审 N2），卡上首位标「Estimates: 650–1,500 kg」（第 3 轮 R3-M1）。

### 备注（口径疑点、来源冲突）

- **#1 African Bush Elephant**：No official species-level population estimate yet: AfESG 2016 Status Report gave ~415,428 for BOTH African species combined (Wikipedia attributes it to bush elephant alone); AfESG savanna report announced for 2026 but not found published. 350,000+ is a partial-coverage floor (GEC 2016). IUCN assessment published 2021 (assessed 2020), amended 2022. Mass cross-check 6,500 vs 6,000 = 8% (ok).
- **#2 Asian Elephant**：Global figure likely overstated now: it uses India 29,964 (2017), but India's 2025 DNA census (https://www.civilsdaily.com/news/status-of-elephants-in-india-report-2025/) gives 22,446 (new method, not directly comparable) -> global total may now be ~40,000-45,000 (inference). Mass cross-check SDZG 3,600 vs 4,000 = 10% (borderline); Owen-Smith 2024 gives 4,000-5,400. Larramendi notes many published Asian elephant weights come from obese captive animals. IUCN assessed 18 Sep 2019, published 2020. Captive 'up to 6,000 kg' (SDZG) not used.
- **#3 White Rhinoceros**：Northern white rhino (C. s. cottoni): only 2 left (Kenya). IUCN 2020: 10,080 mature individuals; assessed 6 Jan 2020. No reliable individual max weight found (ADW range up to 3,600 kg; Wikipedia's 4,500 kg unsourced). ~1,299 more in zoos outside Africa. Shoulder height 1.70-1.85 m (SDZG, both sexes). Daily Maverick: SA white rhinos -15% in 2024 (14,074 incl. other counts).
- **#4 Indian Rhinoceros**：IUCN assessed 6 Dec 2018 (published 2019): 2,100-2,200 mature individuals. Nepal figure 752 is from the 2021 count; 2025 count postponed, 2026 DNA-based count planned (no result found). ADW shoulder height males 1.70-1.86 m (midpoint used). No reliable individual max weight found.
- **#5 African Forest Elephant**：MASS CONFLICT >10%: Larramendi 2016 ~2.0 t vs Owen-Smith 2024 table 4.5 t vs Grubb et al. 2000 (via Wikipedia) 4-7 t for bulls. No direct weighings exist; highly uncertain. Female mass not reliably sourced (Owen-Smith 2,500 kg) -> null. Population rise vs 2016 reflects better survey coverage, not growth (IUCN). IUCN assessed 13 Nov 2020, published 2021.
- **#6 Hippopotamus**：Mass: Owen-Smith 2024 range 1,500-2,600 looks like mean-to-max, so its midpoint (2,050) would overstate (>10% from 1,546). Max conflict: Kingdon 3,200 kg vs Klingel ~2,065 kg vs Wikipedia 2,660 kg. Zambia/Tanzania counts dated; range states argued at CITES CoP19 that IUCN 2017 underestimated several countries. Trend 'stable' per Wikipedia summary of IUCN 2017, not verified on IUCN page. Height 1.50-1.65 m (SDZG, Kingdon 1979; labelled height, not explicitly shoulder). Value 122,500 = midpoint.
- **#7 Javan Rhinoceros**：MASS CONFLICT >10%: Owen-Smith 1,300 kg vs ADW 1,500-2,000 kg vs Handbook of Mammals of the World 1,200-1,500 kg (search snippet only); few wild weighings, ADW notes females may be larger. IUCN: CR, Ellis & Talukdar 2020 (seen on Rhino Resource Center author list); exact Red List URL/assessment ID and trend not verified (iucnredlist.org returned 403). Population conflict: IRF 2022 high estimate 76; an Indonesian 2026 news snippet (rri.co.id) mentions 80-100 - unverified; CITES/IUCN 2025 figure ~50 used. Taxonomy: Nardelli & Heissig (ZooKeys 2025) proposed genus Eurhinoceros (E. sondaicus) - a proposal, not adopted by IUCN.
- **#8 Giraffe**：TAXONOMY: IUCN SSC Giraffe & Okapi SG (Aug 2025) recognised 4 species (Northern G. camelopardalis, Reticulated G. reticulata, Masai G. tippelskirchi, Southern G. giraffa) and 7 subspecies; Red List still lists one species (VU, 2016 assessment amended 2018, ~97,500 animals then); per-species Red List assessments planned for 2026 (CMS doc). If the split is used, the ranking entry may need to be one species (e.g. Southern giraffe, most numerous). Trend 'decreasing' per 2016 listing rationale (~36-40% decline 1985-2015, media), not read on IUCN page; GCF 2025 says 3 of 4 species now stable/increasing. Max mass: Larramendi 2016 says largest bulls ~1.2-1.5 t (no individual record) -> null; 1,511.6 kg (Mitchell et al.) and 1,900 kg claims not verified. Stronghold uncertain: figures from different years/methods.

IUCN assessed 14 Jan 2020: 3,142 mature individuals; subspecies D. b. bicornis downlisted to NT. Sexes similar in size. SDZG gives 1,000-1,800 kg. No reliable max record.
