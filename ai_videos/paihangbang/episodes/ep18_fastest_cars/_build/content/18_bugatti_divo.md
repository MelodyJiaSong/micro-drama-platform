## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Chiron-based, handling-focused coachbuilt model; limited to 380 km/h (236 mph) because of extra downforce and tyre load; no top-speed key. 40 built 2019-2021.

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
Bugatti with aggressive aero: large fixed rear wing, NACA roof duct, vertical-fin tail lights, often French Racing Blue and grey 'Titanium Liquid Silver'.
