## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Runs of 427.933 and 434.211 km/h at Ehra-Lessien (2010), two-way average 431.072 km/h witnessed by Guinness and TÜV. Customer cars limited to 415 km/h; Guinness later questioned the record because the limiter was off. Veyron family (16.4, Grand Sport, Super Sport, Vitesse) = one model.

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
Rounded Bugatti Veyron coupe, Super Sport has orange-and-black carbon 'World Record Edition' livery, roof NACA ducts, horseshoe grille.
