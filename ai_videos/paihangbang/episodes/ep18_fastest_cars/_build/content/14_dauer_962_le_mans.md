# #14 Dauer 962 Le Mans

- 品牌国 / 国旗：Germany（DE）
- 口径：VERIFIED（独立计时实测，单向最高）；主值 404.6 km/h（251 mph）
- 年份：1998；产量：~13 built；动力：Flat-6
- 主源：[Auto Bild, 2003-06-26: Le Mans ist überall (Dauer 962 LM, 404.6 km/h at Ehra-Lessien)](http://www.autobild.de/artikel/dauer-962-le-mans-42071.html)
- 交叉核对：[Wikipedia: Dauer 962 Le Mans (404.6 km/h independently measured, 1998)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dauer_962_Le_Mans)

## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Road-registered version of the Porsche 962 Group C racer built by Dauer Sportwagen (own manufacturer); 251.4 mph (404.6 km/h) independently measured at Ehra-Lessien in Nov 1998 (Evo Sept 2003, Auto Bild). Number built: at least 10 (about 13). A TÜV report quoted elsewhere gives 402 km/h; both figures exist. Period press measurement (Auto Bild / Evo), timing body not stated.

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
Road-legal Porsche 962 Le Mans racer: long low Group C body, enclosed rear wheels, big rear wing, often white with Dauer/Porsche livery.

## 易混淆对象（找图要排除）
Racing Porsche 962C (race numbers, sponsors) — prefer road-registered Dauer car; not Porsche 911 GT1.
