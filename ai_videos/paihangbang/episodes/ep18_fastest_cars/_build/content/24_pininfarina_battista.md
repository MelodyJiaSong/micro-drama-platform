## 它是什么 / 记录细节
358.03 km/h on 2023-02-15 at NATRAX high-speed track (Pithampur, India), driven by Autocar India editor Hormazd Sorabjee, records validated by FMSCI. Maker's rated top speed 350 km/h. Brand origin Italy (Pininfarina, Turin); Automobili Pininfarina is HQ'd in Munich and owned by India's Mahindra; car built on Rimac technology.

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
Italian-designed electric hypercar, smooth sculpted body with light bar front, rear 'floating' light blade and large diffuser, butterfly doors.
