# #1 Yangwang U9 Xtreme

- 品牌国 / 国旗：China（CN）
- 口径：VERIFIED（独立计时实测，单向最高）；主值 496.22 km/h（308 mph）
- 年份：2025；产量：30 planned；动力：EV
- 主源：[BYD / Yangwang press release, 2025-09 (ATP Papenburg certified)](https://www.byd.com/mea/news-list/yangwang-u9-xtreme-is-the-worlds-fastest-production-car-with-top-speed-of-496kmh)
- 交叉核对：[Wikipedia: List of production car speed records (excluded list, one-way run)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_production_car_speed_records)

## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Record car described as a prototype in Wikipedia's list (status not stated by BYD). First customer Xtreme delivered 2026-04-24 at Auto China (CnEVPost). 496.22 km/h on 2025-09-14 at ATP Automotive Testing Papenburg, driver Marc Basseng, one direction only (so not a Guinness two-way record). Xtreme = track-focused limited version of the U9 (max 30 units); U9 Track Edition specs filed with China's MIIT (road homologation filing). Standard U9 (on sale since Feb 2024, mass production Aug 2024) reached 391.94 km/h in 2024. Brand: Yangwang (BYD), China.

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
Low, wide two-door electric supercar coupe (BYD's Yangwang brand). Xtreme version: big fixed rear wing, front splitter, track livery often dark grey / black with racing decals; standard U9 has scissor doors, X-shaped LED tail light, 'Yangwang' gate-shaped logo.
