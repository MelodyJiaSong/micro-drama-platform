- 品牌国 / 国旗：United States（US）
- 口径：VERIFIED（独立计时实测，单向最高）；主值 474.76 km/h（295 mph）

- 主源：[Hagerty UK, 2022-05-26: SSC Tuatara hits 295 mph (dual Racelogic VBOX)](https://www.hagerty.co.uk/articles/news-articles/ssc-tuatara-hypercar-hits-295mph-and-this-time-its-for-real)
- 交叉核对：[Wikipedia: List of production car speed records (2-way avg 282.9 mph = 455.3 km/h, 2021)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_production_car_speed_records)

## 它是什么 / 记录细节
295 mph (474.8 km/h) one-way on 2022-05-14 at Johnny Bohmer Proving Grounds (ex-Shuttle runway), owner Larry Caplin in chassis #001; two Racelogic VBOX units installed by Racelogic's technician, independent observer Robert Mitchell. Two-way average 282.9 mph (455.3 km/h) on 2021-01-17 at Kennedy Space Center. The 2020 claim of 316/331 mph was withdrawn by SSC and is NOT used. Cross-check differs 4.1% because it is the two-way average (different measure).

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
Long, low US hypercar with jet-fighter canopy, very long tapered tail, small rear wing, butterfly doors; SSC North America, Richland, Washington.
