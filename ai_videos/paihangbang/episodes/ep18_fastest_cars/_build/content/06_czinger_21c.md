## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Maker figure 281 mph (452 km/h) given in Czinger's 2021 production-spec release for the optional low-drag (V Max) configuration; not independently run. When the V Max was shown in 2022, Autocar / Auto Express quoted 253 mph (407 km/h); both figures exist. 80 units is the total for all 21C variants. 80 units planned (all variants); customer deliveries under way (H.R. Owen delivered first UK customer car). 3D-printed chassis, tandem seating, hybrid 2.9 V8, 1,250 hp.

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
American hypercar with tandem (one-behind-the-other) seating, fighter-jet canopy, slim body, 3D-printed structure, large rear wing on standard car.
