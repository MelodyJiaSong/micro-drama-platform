## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Peak 391 km/h (243 mph), two-way average 240.1 mph (386.4 km/h), 1998-03-31, Andy Wallace, prototype XP5 with rev limiter raised to 8,300 rpm. Wikipedia lists 221 mph (355.7 km/h) for an unmodified car. Still the fastest naturally aspirated production car. 64 road cars (106 F1s total).

## 长什么样（辨认特征）
1990s McLaren with central driving seat, roof snorkel intake, gold-foil engine bay, butterfly doors, compact rounded body.
