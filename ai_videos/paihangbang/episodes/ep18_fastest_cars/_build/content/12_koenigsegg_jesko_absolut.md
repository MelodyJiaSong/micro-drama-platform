## 它是什么 / 记录细节
Peak of 412 km/h reached during the 0-400-0 km/h record run, 2024-06-27, airfield in Örebro, Sweden; data recorded with Racelogic equipment and verified by Racelogic HQ (UK). It is an acceleration-test peak, not a flat-out top-speed run; Koenigsegg quotes a simulated 531 km/h but has not run it. Jesko family (Attack / Absolut, 125 total) counted once; deliveries since 2022-23. Aug 2025 run improved 0-400-0 to 25.21 s (no peak published).
