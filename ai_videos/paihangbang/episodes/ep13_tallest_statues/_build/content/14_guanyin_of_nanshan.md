# 13 · Guanyin of Nanshan（China）

- 位置：Sanya, Hainan, China
- 本体高：78 m（256 ft）；含基座总高：108 m；建成：2005
- 主源：[Sina News, 25 Apr 2005: 南山海上观音 高108米 开光](https://news.sina.cn/sa/2005-04-25/detail-ikkntiam3335350.d.html)
- 全景自由：ok

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
white three-faced Guanyin statue standing on a man-made island in the sea off Sanya, Hainan, connected by a long bridge

## 标志性视角 / 最佳机位
full statue from the shore or bridge with sea and sky, or aerial; must show three faces / sea setting

## 易混淆对象（找图时排除）
Lady Buddha Da Nang, Tsz Shan Guanyin, Xiqiao Guanyin

## 调研笔记
Three-faced white Guanyin on an artificial island. 108 m includes the 108-petal lotus seat and the Vajra platform that houses a hall; the figure-only 78 m is from WPL (no independent breakdown found) — flagged for review.

## 维基百科摘要（只作文字参考，图片不用维基）
The Guanyin of Nanshan (Chinese: 南山海上观音圣像) is a 108-metre (354 ft) statue of the bodhisattva Guanyin, sited on the south coast of the Island with Hainan on top of the Nanshan Temple of Sanya.
