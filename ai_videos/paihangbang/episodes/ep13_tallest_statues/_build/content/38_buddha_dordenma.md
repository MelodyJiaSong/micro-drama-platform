- 本体高：54 m（177 ft）；含基座总高：— m；建成：2015

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
gilded bronze seated Shakyamuni Buddha on the Kuensel Phodrang hill above Thimphu, Bhutan, on a temple base with golden female deity statues around the plaza
