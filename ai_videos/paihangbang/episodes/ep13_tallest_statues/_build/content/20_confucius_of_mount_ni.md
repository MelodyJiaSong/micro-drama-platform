# 18 · Confucius of Mount Ni（China）

- 位置：Nishan, Qufu, Shandong, China
- 本体高：72 m（236 ft）；含基座总高：— m；建成：2016
- 主源：[Huanqiu, 2016: 尼山72米铜质孔子像落成](https://m.huanqiu.com/article/9CaKrnK2g6K)
- 全景自由：ok

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
huge bronze standing Confucius with hands folded, in scholar robes, at Nishan Shengjing park near Qufu, Shandong

## 标志性视角 / 最佳机位
full figure from the axial avenue, clear sky

## 易混淆对象（找图时排除）
small Confucius statues in Qufu temple, Confucius statues abroad

## 调研笔记
Bronze Confucius at Nishan Shengjing; 72 m for his 72 leading disciples; built from May 2012, unveiled 2016.
