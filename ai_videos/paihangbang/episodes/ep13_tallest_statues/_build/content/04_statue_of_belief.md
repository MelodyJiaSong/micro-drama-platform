# 12 · Statue of Belief（India）

- 位置：Nathdwara, Rajasthan, India
- 本体高：78.9 m（259 ft）；含基座总高：112.5 m；建成：2022
- 主源：[The Daily Guardian: 112 m (369 ft) overall incl. a 34 m (110 ft) pedestal; Wikipedia 'Statue of Belief' same split](https://www.magzter.com/nl/stories/newspaper/The-Daily-Guardian/STANDING-TALL-IN-FAITH-THE-STATUE-OF-BELIEF-IN-NATHDWARA)
- 全景自由：ok

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
huge copper-coloured seated Shiva in meditation, holding a trident, with a snake around the neck, on Ganesh Tekri hill at Nathdwara, Rajasthan

## 标志性视角 / 最佳机位
full seated figure from the front, ideally at dusk with lighting or clear blue sky

## 易混淆对象（找图时排除）
Murudeshwar Shiva (sea, Karnataka), Adiyogi bust (Coimbatore), Kailashnath Mahadev (Nepal), Mangal Mahadev (Mauritius)

## 调研笔记
Shiva ('Vishwas Swaroopam'), unveiled 29 Oct 2022. Overall 369 ft (112.5 m) incl. a 110 ft (33.5 m) pedestal (Wikipedia article; Daily Guardian: '34 m high pedestal, reaches 112 m'), so the seated figure is 369 - 110 = 259 ft = 78.9 m (derived). The 351 ft (107 m) in the NBM&CW builder report was the overall height during construction, before the Ganga stream was added to the hair (Rajasthan Tour Planner / Zee News). WPL lists 106 m statue / 112 m total, which treats the pedestal as only 6 m; contradicted by the 110 ft pedestal and the 110 ft viewing gallery. Previously ranked No. 4 in our draft.

## 维基百科摘要（只作文字参考，图片不用维基）
Vishwas Swaroopam (also known as Statue of Belief) is a statue of the Hindu god Shiva constructed at Nathdwara in Rajasthan, India. It opened on 29 October 2022. Vishwas Swaroopam is the tallest statue of Shiva in the world and 4th tallest statue worldwide.
