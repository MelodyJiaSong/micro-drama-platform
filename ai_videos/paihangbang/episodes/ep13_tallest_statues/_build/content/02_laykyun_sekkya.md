# 2 · Laykyun Sekkya（Myanmar）

- 位置：Monywa, Sagaing, Myanmar
- 本体高：116 m（380 ft）；含基座总高：129.2 m；建成：2008
- 主源：[OrangeSmile: Laykyun Setkyar (116 m on a 13.5 m throne)](https://www.orangesmile.com/extreme/en/largest-monuments/laykyun-setkyar.htm)
- 全景自由：risk（FoP: Myanmar Copyright Law 2019 has no freedom of panorama）

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
huge golden-yellow standing Buddha with red robe accents on a hill at Bodhi Tataung near Monywa, a giant reclining Buddha lies in front of it

## 标志性视角 / 最佳机位
full standing figure from below on the hill path, or a wide shot with the reclining Buddha in the foreground

## 易混淆对象（找图时排除）
the reclining Buddha alone, Kyaikhto seated Buddha, Shwedagon

## 调研笔记
Standing Buddha on a 13.41 m lotus throne (129.2 m total). Some sources round to 116 m.

## 维基百科摘要（只作文字参考，图片不用维基）
The Laykyun Sekkya Buddha (Burmese: လေးကျွန်းစကြာ) is a colossal statue depicting the Buddha which is located at the Monywa town of Sagaing Region. As of May 2026, it is the third-tallest statue in the world at 116 metres (381 ft).
