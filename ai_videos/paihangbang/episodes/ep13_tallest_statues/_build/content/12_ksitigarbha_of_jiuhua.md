# 10 · Ksitigarbha of Jiuhua（China）

- 位置：Mount Jiuhua, Anhui, China
- 本体高：84 m（276 ft）；含基座总高：99 m；建成：2013
- 主源：[Sina News, 31 Aug 2013: 九华山地藏菩萨圣像开光 (像身84米, 加莲花座99米)](https://news.sina.cn/sa/2013-08-31/detail-ikmxzfmk2416114.d.html)
- 全景自由：ok

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
huge bronze standing Ksitigarbha (Dizang) monk with shaved head, holding a tall khakkhara staff in the right hand and a jewel in the left, on a lotus base at Dayuan Cultural Park, Mount Jiuhua, Anhui

## 标志性视角 / 最佳机位
full statue from the long axial plaza and stairs in front

## 易混淆对象（找图时排除）
Jiuhua mountain temples, Spring Temple Buddha, Lingshan Buddha

## 调研笔记
Bronze Dizang (Ksitigarbha) at Dayuan Cultural Park; consecrated 31 Aug 2013. Body 84 m, 99 m with lotus base (139 m 'viewing height' incl. platform). WPL gives 76 m [citation needed] and completion 2012 — differs >10%; we use the 2013 news figure.
