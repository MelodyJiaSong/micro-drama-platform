# 22 · Yang'asha Statue（China）

- 位置：Jianhe, Guizhou, China
- 本体高：66 m（217 ft）；含基座总高：88 m；建成：2017
- 主源：[People's Daily app / The Paper, Oct 2020: 剑河88米仰阿莎雕塑 (像高66米, 底座22米)](https://www.peopleapp.com/rmharticle/30018156007)
- 全景自由：ok

## 长什么样 / 辨认特征
stainless-steel statue of the Miao goddess Yang'asha wearing silver headdress and holding a moon, on a round base by a river in Jianhe county, Guizhou

## 标志性视角 / 最佳机位
full figure from the riverside, silver metallic shine

## 易混淆对象（找图时排除）
other Miao village sculptures, silver jewelry close-ups

## 调研笔记
Stainless-steel Miao goddess of beauty; 66 m figure on a 22 m base. Built Nov 2016–Jul 2017; criticised in 2020 amid China's curbs on giant statues.
