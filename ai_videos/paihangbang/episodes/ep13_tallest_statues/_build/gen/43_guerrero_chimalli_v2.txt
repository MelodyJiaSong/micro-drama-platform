The Guerrero Chimalli monument in Chimalhuacán, State of Mexico: a colossal cubist sculpture of an Aztec warrior made entirely of flat, faceted bright red steel plates. Its body is a tall, narrow, blocky column of angular planes; the head is a tall crown of sharp pointed triangular spikes; on the chest is a huge round shield shaped like a spiky star or cog, built from many pointed triangular red facets radiating outward; the right arm rises straight up beside the head holding a tall rectangular block-shaped club; a box-like rectangular balcony juts out sideways at shoulder height; the legs are angular red blocks. It stands on a short square white pedestal with red square panels, in the middle of the median of a wide, straight multi-lane avenue, with low-rise colourful concrete houses spreading to the hills and a volcano on the horizon.
