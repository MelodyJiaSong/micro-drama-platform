## 说明
Icon of the Seas (2023 delivery, Jan 2024 debut), Star of the Seas (2025), Legend of the Seas (2026) all registered at 248,663 GT (DNV). Royal Caribbean marketing figure 250,800 GT is not the registered tonnage.

## 维基摘要（只作文字参考）
The Icon class is a class of cruise ships ordered by Royal Caribbean International to be built by Meyer Turku in Turku, Finland. As of 2026, this class is the largest cruise ship class ever constructed. Royal Caribbean plans to have at least seven Icon-class ships, which will include Icon of the Seas, Star of the Seas, Legend of the Seas, and Hero of the Seas. Furthermore, Royal Caribbean has announced orders for additional three ships which are expected to enter service in 2028, 2029, and 2030 respectively.

## 长什么样（找图辨认）
Royal Caribbean cruise ship, 365 m; white hull with blue crown-and-anchor funnel; a giant glass 'AquaDome' at the front of the top decks; colourful water-slide park (Category 6) at the stern.

## 易混淆对象
Not an Oasis-class ship (no glass dome, open-air 'Central Park' slot). Sisters Star of the Seas (2025) and Legend of the Seas (2026) look almost identical.

## 最佳拍摄视角 / 地点
Three-quarter bow or broadside view at sea, whole ship visible.
