## 维基摘要（只作文字参考）
The Evergreen A class is a series of 13 container ships built for Evergreen Marine. The largest ships have a maximal theoretical capacity of around 24,004 TEU and are among the largest container ships in the world. Six ships were built by Samsung Heavy Industries in South Korea, and another seven were built by China State Shipbuilding Corporation (CSSC) at two shipyards in China.
