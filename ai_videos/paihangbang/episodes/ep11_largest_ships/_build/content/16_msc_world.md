## 说明

## 维基摘要（只作文字参考）
World Class ships are a class of cruise ships being built by Chantiers de l'Atlantique of France for MSC Cruises. The lead vessel, MSC World Europa, was delivered in October 2022 and the second, MSC World America, in March 2025. Six more ships in this class are planned for delivery between 2026 and 2031.

## 长什么样（找图辨认）
MSC Cruises LNG cruise ship (2022-26), 333 m; white hull with blue wave graphics on the bow; distinctive Y-shaped stern with a dry slide 'The Venom Drop'; very tall superstructure.
