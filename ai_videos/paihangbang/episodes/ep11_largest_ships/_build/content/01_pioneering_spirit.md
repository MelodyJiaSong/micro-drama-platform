## 说明
Twin-hull platform installation/decommissioning vessel. Hull built by DSME (South Korea), 2014; completed in the Netherlands and in service since 2016. Allseas spec sheet: 382 m (477 m incl. stinger) x 124 m, topsides lift 48,000 t.

## 维基摘要（只作文字参考）
Pioneering Spirit, formerly Pieter Schelte, is a split hull crane vessel owned by Allseas designed for the single-lift installation and removal of large oil and gas platforms, and pipelines. The 382-metre-long (1,253 ft), 124-metre-wide (407 ft) vessel is the world's largest vessel by gross tonnage, the heaviest vehicle ever made and since September 2021 also the largest floating sheerleg in the world. It was built in South Korea by Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering in 2013 at a cost of €2.6 billion. It commenced offshore operations in August 2016.

## 长什么样（找图辨认）
Twin-hull (split-hull) crane vessel, 382 m x 124 m; a 122 m-long slot cut into the bow between the two hulls; eight huge yellow topsides-lift beams at the bow; white/grey superstructure and helideck at the stern; 'ALLSEAS' on the hull.

## 易混淆对象
Not Allseas' pipelayers Solitaire/Audacia; not Heerema's semi-submersible crane vessels Sleipnir or Thialf (red cranes on columns); not the Prelude FLNG.
