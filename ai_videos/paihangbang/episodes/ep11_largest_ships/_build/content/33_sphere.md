## 维基摘要（只作文字参考）
The Sphere-class cruise ship is a class of cruise ships that entered service with Princess Cruises in February 2024. They are the largest ships built for Princess Cruises and the company's first ships powered by liquefied natural gas (LNG). The first ship is named Sun Princess. Her sister ship, Star Princess (2024), launched in 2024.
