## 维基摘要（只作文字参考）
MSC Gülsün, at the time of her launch in 2019, was the world's largest container ship. Built by Samsung Heavy Industries in South Korea, she is 61.5 metres wide and 399.9 metres long. With a cargo system designed by MacGregor International AB, the ship has a capacity of 23,756 containers (TEU) in rows of 24 across. MSC Gülsün is registered in Panama and operated by the Mediterranean Shipping Company based in Geneva, Switzerland and the Netherlands.
