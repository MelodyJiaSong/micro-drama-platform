## 它是什么、在哪
The lake known as O'Higgins in Chile  and San Martín in Argentina is located around coordinates  in Patagonia, between the Aysén Region and the Santa Cruz Province.



## 本期数据
- 最大水深 799 m（Minowa, Schaefer & Skvarca 2023, J. Glaciology 69(278) (CC BY 4.0)）；湖面海拔 252 m（Wikipedia infobox）→ Floor 547 m below sea
- 备注：Long quoted as 836 m (CECS 2003/2010 press figure; Spanish Wikipedia still shows it). The 2023 peer-reviewed survey measured 799 m, 'similar to Schaefer and others (2011)'. Area: Chile 554 km², Argentina 459 km² (Spanish Wikipedia) -> Chile flag.
