## 它是什么、在哪
Grey Lake is a glacially fed lake in Torres del Paine National Park, southern Chile.



## 本期数据
- 最大水深 410 m（Sugiyama et al. 2021, Nature Communications 12, 6301 (CC BY 4.0)）；湖面海拔 45 m（Wikipedia infobox）→ Floor 365 m below sea
- 备注：Fed by Grey Glacier, Southern Patagonian Ice Field.
