## 3. YouTube（英文）

**标题**

```text
-25°C Snow Cave Build | Ice Fishing & Baking Bread on a Hot Stone | Silent Winter Camp
```

**描述**

```text
Two metres of snow. No tools but a shovel, a snow saw and an ice chisel.

I dug down to the ground under a big spruce, hollowed out a room, and punched a
breathing hole through the roof — because a sealed snow cave with a fire in it
will kill you. Snow keeps the heat in. The hole lets the smoke out. You need both.

Then I walked out onto the lake, cut through twenty-five centimetres of ice,
and waited.

What came back: a trout, a pot of snow melted into water, dough kneaded with
frozen hands, and flatbread baked on a hot stone.

No talking. No music except the first and last minute. Just the sound of it.

00:00  The lake
00:26  Into the trees
01:34  Digging down
02:58  Punching the breathing hole
04:06  Ice fishing
05:26  Snow into water
06:06  Baking on stone
06:34  Dinner
07:00  Closing the door
```

```text
snow cave, winter bushcraft, ice fishing, survival shelter, -25 degrees, silent vlog,
```

## 6. 小红书（中文）

**标题**：`-25℃，我在雪里挖了个家，还烤了张饼🥖`

```text
第四集啦～这次是北方针叶林，雪深两米。

📍地点｜冻住的湖 + 一棵大云杉
🏠住的｜树冠下的雪井，向下掏到地面再横着掏出一间屋

最喜欢的两个画面：

全程没有一句话，声音就是旁白。
开头那段几乎是无声的——雪把所有环境音都吃掉了。

#野外生存 #雪洞 #冰钓 #户外美食 #荒野生活 #无人声vlog
```
